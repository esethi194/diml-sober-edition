# A Day in the Life: Sober Edition

A narrative-driven game built in Godot that explores addiction through gameplay mechanics and boss battles.

🔗 Play the game: https://eshasethi194.wixsite.com/diml-sober-edition  
🔗 Play on Itch: https://esethi.itch.io/diml-sober-edition

---

## Overview

This game represents addiction as a dynamic system rather than just a story.

The player faces a central “boss” that spawns smaller enemies representing different substances. Instead of a traditional fight, the goal is to survive and understand the system before using a final mechanic (the mirror shard) to end the cycle.

---

## Features

- Multi-phase boss battle system
- Enemy spawning tied to boss health
- Projectile and collision system
- State-based player and enemy behavior
- Dynamic difficulty through spawning patterns
- Final “mirror shard” mechanic to end each level

## Design Focus

The goal was to make addiction feel like something that builds and surrounds you, rather than something simple or linear.

- The boss never attacks directly — it creates pressure through chaos
- The player doesn’t “win” by overpowering, but by understanding and timing
- The mirror shard represents self-awareness rather than force
